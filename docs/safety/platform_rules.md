# Ficshon Platform Rules

## Platform Position

Ficshon is a user-generated storytelling platform for creating, sharing, and interacting with fictional works across text, audio, and visual formats.

Users create characters, stories, scenes, and worlds. Ficshon hosts this content but does not act as the author or publisher.

---

## Fanfiction Policy

### Allowed

Users may create:

- fanfiction stories
- inspired characters
- alternate universe works
- parody works
- realms themed around existing franchises
- writing inspired by existing worlds

This is treated as user-generated expression.

---

### Not Allowed

Users may not:

- directly reproduce copyrighted text
- claim ownership of existing IP
- upload official artwork or assets
- impersonate official franchise accounts
- sell unlicensed IP content through Ficshon

Ficshon will respond to valid takedown requests.

---

## AI Image & Character Likeness Rules

### Not Allowed

The platform must not intentionally generate:

- exact copyrighted characters
- trademarked character designs
- recognisable celebrity likenesses
- real people without consent

---

### Allowed

Users may generate:

- original characters
- genre-inspired designs
- archetypes (wizard, knight, detective, etc.)
- stylistic inspiration

Ficshon may filter known franchise names and character descriptors in prompts and maintain blocked term lists.

---

## AI Story Generator Rules

### Allowed

- user-directed storytelling
- genre fiction
- romance
- dark themes
- violence within narrative context
- adult themes within platform tiers

---

### Not Allowed

- non-consensual sexual content
- sexual content involving minors
- explicit real-person sexual content
- extremist propaganda
- direct copyrighted text reproduction

The AI acts as a writing assistant guided by the user, not an autonomous publisher.

---

## Writer Workspace Rules

Users may:

- draft stories
- edit content
- store private works
- generate drafts before publishing

Workspace content remains private until posted. Content may be reviewed for policy compliance when published.

---

## Platform Responsibility

Ficshon will:

- provide user controls for content visibility
- respond to reports of violating material
- remove content that breaks platform rules
- suspend accounts when necessary

Users remain responsible for the content they create and publish.

---

## Core Safety Principle

Ficshon enables storytelling.
Users remain responsible for their content.
The platform enforces clear boundaries for legality, consent, and safety.
