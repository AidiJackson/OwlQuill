# Ficshon Age & Content Policy

## Age Requirement

Ficshon is intended for users aged 18 and over only.

By creating an account, users confirm:

- they are at least 18 years old
- they understand the platform may contain mature or adult storytelling content
- they accept responsibility for their interactions and content

Ficshon does not knowingly allow minors to use the platform.
Accounts suspected to belong to under-18 users may be suspended or removed.

---

## Content Tier System

Ficshon uses a tiered content visibility model so users can control what they see.

---

### Tier 1 — General Fiction

Allowed content:

- adventure
- fantasy
- drama
- humour
- romance without explicit sexual detail
- standard narrative violence

Visibility:

- shown by default to all users

---

### Tier 2 — Mature Fiction

Allowed content:

- darker themes
- emotional intensity
- implied sexual intimacy
- strong violence within narrative context
- disturbing or psychological themes

Visibility:

- shown by default
- may be optionally hidden via user settings

---

### Tier 3 — Adult Fiction

Allowed content:

- explicit sexual scenes between consenting adults
- strong horror themes
- explicit violence where narratively justified
- erotic storytelling within platform rules

Visibility:

- hidden by default
- requires explicit user opt-in
- may include click-to-view warnings

---

## Prohibited Content (All Tiers)

The following is not permitted anywhere on Ficshon:

- sexual content involving minors
- non-consensual sexual content
- incest involving minors
- real person sexual depictions
- extremist propaganda
- direct reproduction of copyrighted text
- illegal or exploitative material

---

## Platform Responsibilities

Ficshon will:

- provide user controls for content visibility
- respond to reports of violating material
- remove content that breaks platform rules
- suspend accounts when necessary

Users remain responsible for the content they create and publish.
