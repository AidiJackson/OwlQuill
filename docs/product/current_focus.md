# Ficshon Current Focus

## Phase Position

Ficshon is currently in the **pre-beta stabilisation phase**.

The goal of this phase is to make the platform feel complete, reliable, and impressive for early users arriving through word of mouth.

This phase prioritises experience quality over feature expansion.

---

## Primary Focus Areas

### 1. Character & Identity Experience

The character system is Ficshon's core differentiator.

Current priority:

- maintain reliability of identity lock
- ensure scene generation consistency
- keep visual outputs strong
- minimise friction in the creation flow

The first character experience must remain a strong "wow" moment.

---

### 2. Writing & Creation Tools

Ficshon must immediately feel better than generic social platforms for writing.

Current priority:

- ensure the workspace feels clean and safe
- maintain auto-save reliability
- support readable formatting (headers, italic, bold)
- keep posting flow smooth

Users should feel confident writing on Ficshon from day one.

---

### 3. Social & Interaction Loop

Retention depends on interaction, not just tools.

Current priority:

- ensure posting works reliably
- keep timeline readable and balanced
- maintain stable messaging system
- support realm participation without confusion

The platform should encourage users to return to continue their stories.

---

### 4. Perceived Completeness

Even if some features are basic, the platform should feel like a full ecosystem.

Current priority:

- keep image library reliable
- ensure audio/video features at least function
- maintain a small but meaningful emoji and formatting set
- ensure navigation feels intuitive

The platform should feel finished, not experimental.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals For This Phase

The following are intentionally **not priorities right now**:

- monetisation systems
- advanced analytics
- recommendation algorithms
- mobile apps
- complex moderation dashboards
- major visual rebrand changes
- performance scaling beyond current needs

These will come later.

---

## Success Definition For This Phase

This phase is successful if:

- new users can create something quickly
- the platform feels stable and polished
- writing feels comfortable and supported
- visual storytelling works reliably
- users feel motivated to return to continue their story

---

## Guiding Principle

Ficshon should feel like a real storytelling platform now, even if not every feature is fully developed yet.

Stability, clarity, and creative confidence are more important than adding new tools.
