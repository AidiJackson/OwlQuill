# Ficshon Platform Identity

## Category

Ficshon is a social storytelling platform.

It combines creation, interaction, and storytelling into one system rather than acting as only a writing tool, social network, or AI generator.

---

## Core Function

Ficshon enables users to:

- create characters
- create stories
- generate scenes across text, audio, and visual formats
- publish their work socially
- interact with other creators and readers
- build narrative worlds together

---

## Core Product Loop

Ficshon is built around the loop:

Create → Publish → Interact → Continue the story

Every major feature should strengthen this loop.

---

## Emotional Tone

Ficshon should feel:

- Creative but structured
- Social but not noisy
- Modern but not sterile
- Immersive but not gimmicky
- Respectful to serious writers
- Scalable like a platform rather than niche like a fandom site

---

## Product Guardrails

Ficshon must not drift into being:

- a chaotic social feed
- a meme platform
- a toy AI generator
- a fantasy cosplay niche site
- a diary/blog platform
- a productivity tool only

If a feature pushes Ficshon toward these directions, it should be reconsidered.

---

## Positioning Statement

Ficshon is a social storytelling platform where creators build characters, share stories, and bring worlds to life across text, audio, and visual formats.
